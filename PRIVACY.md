# Privacy Policy

gfgLock has no accounts, no cloud, no telemetry, and no ads. Your files, passwords, keys, and settings never leave your PC. This page lists the one connection the app makes.

## The connection the app makes on its own

- **Updates (GitHub), once a day.** gfgLock asks GitHub whether a newer version exists; the request carries only gfgLock's name and version. When a new version is out, it downloads it from GitHub's releases and installs it when you close gfgLock, after checking the publisher's signature and every file's SHA-256 checksum. Turn the check off during installation, or at any time in Preferences > Updates; with it off, gfgLock connects only when you click **Check now** in Preferences or **Check for updates** in the About dialog.

[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) covers that request.

## What stays on your PC

- The files you encrypt and decrypt, your passwords, and the keys made from them. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Your settings.
- Logs, which are off by default, stay on your PC.
